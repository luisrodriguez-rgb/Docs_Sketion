# What is Sketion?

Sketion is not just a vector drawing board. It is a **Visual Knowledge Workspace** designed for students, developers, and engineers who need to connect ideas, take notes, and solve problems in a spatial and visual manner.

---

## Three Core Pillars

### 1. Infinite Canvas as a Mind Map
Organize your annotations, diagrams, documents, and workflows spatially on an infinite canvas. Free yourself from the linear constraints of classic text editors.

### 2. Built-in Specialized Tools
Sketion unifies flows that previously required multiple distinct applications:
- **PDFs**: Organize and extract text from your documents directly on the board.
- **LaTeX**: Write and render complex mathematical expressions.
- **Mermaid**: Generate flowcharts using clean declarative code.
- **Tables**: Import structured data in CSV formats.
- **Study Mode**: Generate and review study cards (Flashcards) visually.

### 3. Local-First by Design
Your data starts and stays on your device. Sketion runs offline using local databases in your browser (`IndexedDB`). Cloud synchronization via **Supabase** ensures your boards are available across your devices and collaboration rooms without compromising privacy.
